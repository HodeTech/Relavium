## Findings

**Medium — concurrent successful cleanup can throw and leave owned probes.** Both guard
processes can record `parentExisted=false`. A removes its probe and observes the parent;
B removes the last probe and that empty parent; A's subsequent `readdirSync` throws `ENOENT`
outside the accepted-error catch. A falsely fails and leaves its other three probes.
Two isolated processes with delay-only instrumentation reproduced this interleaving: B's
normal cleanup performed the deletion, with no invented filesystem error. The parent verified
the same outcome against `7f7984c8`.

[The guard](../../tools/test-isolation/check.mjs) now directly attempts non-recursive `rmdirSync`
inside the existing catch when the parent was initially absent. The OS checks emptiness
atomically; `ENOTEMPTY`, `EEXIST` and `ENOENT` are accepted. The expanded
[ownership regression](../../tools/test-isolation/check-ownership.mjs) schedules two passing
guards at the real cleanup boundary, expects both to finish successfully and verifies no owned
probe remains. It fails `7f7984c8` with the real `ENOENT` and passes the correction.
