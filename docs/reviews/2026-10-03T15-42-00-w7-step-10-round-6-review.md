and a pin that rejects after cancellation remain clean. The correction checks settlement,

mutation or event production. Six Core and six actual SQLite/CAS regressions are permanent.

finite evidence, not a guarantee over every asynchronous host interleaving.

An uncancellable CAS put can still finish with an unreferenced object. Retained run authority
and storage completion remain distinct; the existing best-effort media-reference and terminal
