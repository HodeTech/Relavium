## Findings and corrections

Runtime/security (`gpt-6-astra`, maximum effort) reproduced a High interruption defect:
while real SQLite retried the decided authorization append, Ctrl-C closed MCP but did not
cancel the engine. The write later completed and credentials/provider egress followed.
Contracts (`gpt-6.1-sol`, extra-high effort) reproduced a separate Medium missing guard:
without MCP, actual SIGINT during asynchronous engine build took Node's default signal exit
instead of the documented exit 1 and pending-gate diagnostic.

The command now owns its cancellation subscription independently of MCP cleanup and forwards
cancellation to the engine and returned handle. The Core façade latches cancellation for a
known passive resume, threads its signal into quote/context preparation and transfers it to
the seeded execution without scheduling before lease adoption. Effect admission observes the
same cancellation intent. Exact-fence renewal still precedes a cancelled terminal; a successor
receives neither a terminal write nor a release. A late successful decision append stays durable:
its approval is never rolled back, and cancellation prevents the later dispatch. Parent permanent
tests also exposed a grace timer armed by passive cancellation; passive checkpoints now arm no
grace backstop, and abandon disarms any existing one.

A Medium display defect let an identified old approval companion clear a newer gate's active
notice at the same node, so a repeated current pause produced another card. Identified companions
now remove only their complete raw run/node/gate identity. The legacy no-id companion keeps its
node-scoped projection. This was a display deduplication defect, not a new financial authority or
an ordinary human prompt. A Low command-reference claim is also corrected: current price validation
occurs after the run lease is acquired and before the gate claim, not before ownership.

The canonical behaviour is in [the command reference](../reference/cli/commands.md#relavium-budget-resume)
and [the runner contract](../reference/shared-core/agent-runner.md#preparing-and-resuming-a-budget-dispatch).
The historical [ADR-0097](../decisions/0097-a-budget-approval-is-an-allowance-not-an-exemption.md)
body is unchanged; a dated correction records the integration repair. No dependency, wire field,
allowance policy, lease TTL or clock rule is added.
