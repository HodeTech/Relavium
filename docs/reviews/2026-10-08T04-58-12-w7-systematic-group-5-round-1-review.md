## Findings and corrections

1. **Failure precedence.** A real exit-17 worker could be hidden by a failing stage evidence write; ten real failed retention children could have their readiness assertion hidden by a writer in `finally`. The production stage establishes its worker/spawn failure before evidence finalization. The shared capture helper attempts every writer, preserves the first evidence-only failure, and contains subsequent failures while the original caller unwinds. Retention uses the same capture after joining every child. Actual caller controls cover each stage process/stdout/stderr writer on failed and successful workers, plus the retention caller itself.
2. **Closed diagnostic pipe.** The synchronous warning guard could not catch asynchronous `process.stderr.write` EPIPE. Fixed secondary diagnostics now use a synchronous descriptor writer. Three real closed-pipe children import the actual check and retain thrown `Error`, `undefined` and `null`, with native exit zero. This proves the actual caller/sink lifetime rather than a throwing callback stub.
3. **Spawn-error completion ordering.** Native ENOENT with no PID reached completion before close. The production stage now captures error separately and joins actual close before its process/evidence record. The permanent control observes real failed-spawn close, null PID and honest failure state. Neither review nor this correction claims the old probe demonstrated an orphaned live process.
4. **Stale retirement pathname.** A same-user mutation after the scan could replace the owner or physical directory before recursive deletion. Retirement now atomically moves a candidate into a fresh private namespace, then compares claimed physical identity and exact owner/completion records with the scanned snapshot. Unexpected claims are preserved and finalization fails; they are never deleted or restored over a successor. Controls cover changed owner, active replacement and a completed replacement with identical owner/completion bytes. This is a namespace-mutation check, not a kernel boundary against a process capable of changing the private claim too. The [harness contract](../../tools/budget-replay-compat/README.md) states that qualification.
5. **Readiness wording.** The two new roadmap paragraphs incorrectly called the isolation marker race IPC. They now say file-marker readiness raced against child closure. Retention concurrency genuinely uses IPC.
