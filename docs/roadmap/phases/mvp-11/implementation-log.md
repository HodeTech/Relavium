### 4.3 — `/doctor` as a report popup, and the deep check's consent (`d4b7c38c`)

The hosts only route. `parseDoctorLine` is recognised where `/permissions` opens its sheet (`submit()` in `chat-ink`; `overlayCommandFor` and the landing in `home-controller`), **before** the line is counted as a submit, which closes 4.1's open item 4 (the gate is unchanged). `openDoctor` loads `doctor-session` and `doctor-popup` by a dynamic import: a 21 KB chunk of its own, measured. Plain, `--json` and one-shot chats get no port and print today's text.

**The consent.** `[d]` arms a line that names the **origins** a key would be sent to, read once from the resolver's pinned destination (`providerEndpointOrigin`: an exact `https:` origin or nothing) and frozen as `{providerId, origin}`. `Ctrl+Y` answers it only through the host's chord step, which is the approval card's own `createChordGuard` with `CHORD_STEP_GUARD`: dwell 800 ms counted from **each acknowledged frame of the line**, the quiet gap, the lockout measured against the key history from before the arming key, and the window; on top of those, a **count** (not a time) of keys that are not navigation keys since the popup opened or the line armed, a dropped `Ctrl+Y` included. A refused `Ctrl+Y` disarms the line and is stamped as a resolution; `Esc`, `Ctrl+C`, a paste and any other key disarm it too. `runConsentedProviderChecks` is given exactly the frozen targets and, in one synchronous step per target, re-reads the adapter and the origin (a changed origin is a `fail` row and nothing is sent) and only then reads the key. One deep run per host (a `WeakMap` on the port's scope), in flight until its report and every retained request have settled; `Esc` on the running step, `Ctrl+C`, a sheet or the host ending aborts it, and the abort reaches the request. An aborted result is never kept; a completed one is kept for the process and shown with its age. MCP rows only report the session (a throwing manager, a throwing transport proxy, a mocked `child_process` and a recording `fetch` prove nothing is contacted). Every label and detail is scrubbed after `validateProviderKey`'s own redaction; `[c]` copies the same rows through the `/copy` path.
